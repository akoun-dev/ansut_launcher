<p><b>ANSUT Launcher</b> is the official home screen for phones distributed by ANSUT (Agence Nationale du Service Universel des Télécommunications), Côte d'Ivoire's national telecom universal service agency.</p><p>Based on AOSP Launcher3 and the open-source Lawnchair project, it delivers a simple, smooth, fully localized experience designed for every user.</p><ul><li><b>ANSUT identity:</b> branded wallpaper and colors.</li><li><b>French interface:</b> every setting is translated.</li><li><b>Ivorian services:</b> essential apps pre-provisioned on the home screen.</li><li><b>Built for entry-level phones:</b> light, fast and data-friendly.</li><li><b>Global search:</b> quick access to apps, contacts and web results from the home screen.</li><li><b>Customization:</b> icon size, grids, icon packs, gestures and more.</li></ul>
